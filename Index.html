Implied Revenue Multiple = Target Asset Valuation / Audited Parent Revenue

  • Audit Defensibility: Traditional physical hardware, logistics, and integration companies generally trade at 0.5x to 1.5x revenue. Elevating this to a conservative 2.06x multiple is justified by the immediate translation of physical assets into reusable digital software components.

Implied P/E Multiple = Target Asset Valuation / Audited Parent Net Profit
